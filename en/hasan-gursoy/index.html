Hasan Gürsoy

Founder · Software developer

Hasan Gürsoy is a Turkish entrepreneur, software developer and digital product developer who founded ALTENFORD STUDIOS and ELFIRA Group.

His work spans web development, user interfaces, custom software and digital products. Through ELFIRA Group, he works on digital experiences and business software; through ALTENFORD STUDIOS, he develops modular software for FiveM roleplay servers.

Who is Hasan Gürsoy?

Hasan Gürsoy is the founder of ALTENFORD STUDIOS, developing independent, modular scripts for the FiveM roleplay ecosystem using QBCore and ox tools. He also founded ELFIRA Group, where his work covers web development, digital design, e-commerce and custom software.

ALTENFORD STUDIOS

The studio develops modular software products for FiveM roleplay servers in the QBCore and ox ecosystem. Its public catalogue, product information and available Tebex checkout links are on the official studio website.
