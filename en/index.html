FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Good to know.

Is domain registration and hosting included?

Domains, paid hosting, and third-party services are quoted separately.

Does the package include an admin dashboard?

Standard packages do not include an admin dashboard. It can be scoped separately.

Why is there no fixed price for custom software?

Dashboards, CRMs, and booking systems differ in features, users, integrations, security, and maintenance needs. We quote after discussing the requirements.

Do you offer support after delivery?

Bug fixes, maintenance, updates, and support can be scoped separately. Duration, service levels, and fees are confirmed in writing.

Are payment and shipping integrations included?

Payment providers, delivery partners, and integrations are discussed during discovery. Third-party accounts and service fees are separate.

Are monthly maintenance plans subscriptions?

These are indicative monthly service models. Terms, scope, invoicing, and cancellation are agreed in writing. No subscription starts automatically on this website.

How long does a project take?

Timing depends on the agreed scope and supplied content. We set a schedule at project kickoff.

Do you guarantee a Google ranking?

No. We can implement a technical SEO foundation, but search rankings cannot be guaranteed.
